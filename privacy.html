Albumatic

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Albumatic app for Android and iPhone.

In short: Albumatic collects no data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. On Android the app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. On iPhone it sends nothing anywhere either; the only network use is iOS fetching one of your own photos from your iCloud when you open or share a photo that isn't stored on your iPhone.

What the app reads

With your permission, Albumatic reads your photos (on Android, the ones in the Camera folder): when each was taken, where (from the location stored in the photo) and a preview to show in albums. On iPhone it also checks whether a photo without a location has camera details, to leave out screenshots and saved images. Albumatic never changes, moves or deletes your photos. Android gives it read-only access; iOS has no read-only setting, so the app simply contains no code that changes photos, and iOS asks you before any app does.

What stays on your phone

Your home country, settings, the albums you make and your changes to trips, plus a list of your photos' dates and countries, in the app's own private storage. Countries are worked out on your phone from maps bundled with the app. None of this leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it all; your photos stay where they are.

When you share

“Share” (and “Open in…” on Android) hands a photo to the app you pick, such as a messaging app. That app gets a copy of, or read access to, that photo and works under its own privacy policy. On iPhone, the copy made for sharing is kept in the app's temporary storage and deleted when the share sheet closes.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app. We don't sell or share anything, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
