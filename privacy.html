Albumatic

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Albumatic app for Android.

In short: Albumatic collects no data. It has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app reads

With your permission, Albumatic reads the photos in your phone's Camera folder: when each was taken, where (from the GPS location stored in the photo) and a small preview to show in albums. Android gives it read-only access: it can't change, move or delete your photos.

What stays on your phone

Your home country, settings, the albums you make and your changes to trips, plus a list of your photos' dates and countries, in the app's own private storage. Countries are worked out on your phone from maps bundled with the app. None of this leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it all; your photos stay where they are.

When you share

“Open in…” and “Share” hand a photo to the app you pick, such as your Gallery or a messaging app. That app gets read access to that photo and works under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app. We don't sell or share anything, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
