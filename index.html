Albumatic

Your photos are never moved, edited or deleted.

Albumatic sorts the photos in your phone's Camera folder into albums by itself: one for each month at home, and one for each trip abroad. The albums exist only inside the app.

Help

How albums are made

  • Photos taken in your home country go into one album per month.
  • Photos taken in another country become a trip, such as “UK Sep–Oct 2026”. A trip ends when you take a photo at home, or after a break of more than 7 days without photos (change this under Settings → Trip gap).
  • One or two photos at an airport on the way (a stopover) join the trip next to them.
  • A photo without a location takes the country of the photos taken within 12 hours of it. If there are none, it goes to Unknown location.
  • The country comes from the photo's GPS location, looked up on your phone. Albumatic has no internet access.

Changing a trip

Open a trip and tap ⋮ to rename it, merge it with the trip before or after, or undo your changes. Hold a photo to select it, then start a new trip at that photo, move photos to another trip, share them or add them to one of your own albums. New photos never undo your changes.

Why are some photos missing?

  • Albumatic only looks at the Camera folder (DCIM/Camera). Screenshots and photos saved from WhatsApp or other apps aren't included.
  • On Android 14 and later, if you chose “Allow limited access”, Albumatic sees only the photos you picked. Tap “Allow all photos” on the albums screen.
  • If trips are all in Unknown location, allow access to photo locations in Android Settings → Apps → Albumatic → Permissions → Photos and videos.

Is it safe for my photos?

Yes, by design. Android gives Albumatic read-only access, so it can't change, move or delete a photo even by mistake. Removing an album in Albumatic removes only the album; your photos stay where they are. Uninstalling the app removes its albums and nothing else. “Open in…” and “Share” pass a photo to the app you choose, which then works under its own rules.
